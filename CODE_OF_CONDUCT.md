# Code of Conduct

Fit is a small project; the rules are correspondingly simple.

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language — in issues, pull requests, reviews, or commit messages.
- Keep discussions technical and on topic. Disagreements get resolved with reasoning and, where possible, reproducible evidence.
- Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates these rules, and may block repeat offenders.

To report a problem, email temma.ai.brain@gmail.com or use GitHub's built-in reporting tools. Reports are handled confidentially.
